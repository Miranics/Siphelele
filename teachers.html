Common questions

Do learners need data?

No. After the app is installed, lessons and the AI tutor work without data or Wi-Fi. A connection is only needed to download new lessons.

Which grades and subjects?

Grades 10 to 12. The first release is planned to cover Mathematics, Physical Sciences, Computer Literacy and English and isiXhosa support.

Is it free for schools?

Pricing has not been decided. Imfundo Quest is still a proposal.

Is Imfundo Quest available now?

Not yet. Imfundo Quest is a proposed app, and this website is a university project prototype.
