The gap is connection, not phones.

Most South African households own a mobile phone. In the Eastern Cape, far fewer can reach public Wi-Fi.

Why Imfundo Quest

Learners have phones. What they often don’t have is data, Wi-Fi or steady power. Imfundo Quest is built around that reality.

Works offline

Lessons and the AI tutor run on the phone itself, so learning continues during load shedding and in low-signal areas.

Learn through play

Short, game-like lessons with points and levels keep learners motivated, a few minutes at a time.

How it works

Three steps from install to progress.

  1. Get the app

    Install it once, then use it without data.

  2. Learn in short bursts

    Pick a subject, play a micro-lesson and ask the AI tutor when you’re stuck.

  3. Level up

    Earn points, unlock new levels and see your progress.

I know this problem first-hand

In 2015, I travelled to the Eastern Cape for school holiday classes and found there was no internet to do my homework. Imfundo Quest is the tutor I wish I had.
