How a lesson works

Every micro-lesson follows the same four-step loop.

  1. Learn

    A short explanation, 3 minutes or less.

  2. Play

    A quick game to practise the idea.

  3. Earn

    Points and badges for finishing.

  4. Level up

    Unlock the next challenge.

No data? No problem.

Lessons, games and the AI tutor run on the phone. A connection is only needed to get new lessons.

Works without data

  • Lessons and games
  • AI tutor hints and explanations
  • Points, badges and levels

Needs a connection once in a while

  • Installing the app
  • Downloading new lessons and updates
