About Imfundo Quest

A business plan for an offline AI tutor app: a gamified educational app for high school learners in Idutywa, Eastern Cape. Imfundo means education in isiXhosa.

Our story

I grew up in the city. In 2015, I travelled to the Eastern Cape for school holiday classes and found there was no internet to do my homework.

That stayed with me. Many lessons now assume a connection, and learners in places like Idutywa often have a phone but no data, Wi-Fi or steady power. Imfundo Quest is the tutor I wish I had: a game-like app that runs on the phone itself.

The core problem and market opportunity

The core problem

While South Africa’s national internet penetration has steadily expanded in most high schools, the rural town of Idutywa, and the Eastern Cape Province as a whole, still faces severe exclusion because of a multi-tiered digital divide. Systemic infrastructure failure separates the key stakeholders:

Primary stakeholders

Students

Rural students are denied a fair chance at modern academic progression.

Secondary stakeholders

Under-resourced teachers

Teachers work without the connection and tools that modern teaching assumes.

Tertiary stakeholders

Households barely scraping by

High data costs are hard to carry for households with little money to spare.

As a result, an ongoing educational gap denies rural students a fair chance at modern academic progression.

The market opportunity

For Imfundo Quest, this regional inequality represents a vital business and market opportunity.

The lack of reliable broadband and the high cost of data create clear demand for a local, data-free educational solution. By building a gamified, offline AI tutor app that works without an active internet connection, Imfundo Quest serves an unserved rural student demographic.

Our values

Access first

Learning should not depend on a connection.

Built for Idutywa

Designed with local learners and teachers, not for them.

Learning that’s fun

Games that help learners stay motivated.

Meet the founder

Siphelele is an Entrepreneurial Leadership student at African Leadership University and the founder of Imfundo Quest.

A school holiday trip to the Eastern Cape in 2015 showed how hard it is to study without internet. Siphelele wants to build the tutor that was missing, together with learners and teachers in Idutywa.
