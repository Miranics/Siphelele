About FemConnect

The story and mission behind an offline tutor for rural learners.

Our story

I grew up in the city. In 2015, I travelled to the Eastern Cape for school holiday classes and found there was no internet to do my homework.

That stayed with me. Many lessons now assume a connection, and learners in places like Idutywa often have a phone but no data, Wi-Fi or steady power. FemConnect is the tutor I wish I had: a game-like app that runs on the phone itself.

The problem we’re solving

Connectivity, cost and power gaps keep rural learners from digital learning.

Connectivity

In 2023, only 1.6% of Eastern Cape households had access to public Wi-Fi, the lowest of any province. Load shedding adds more interruptions.

Cost

Mobile data is a recurring cost. Learners who rely on it for schoolwork can run out before the work is done.

Language and support

Learners often switch between isiXhosa and English, and many teachers have had little ICT training.

Our values

Access first

Learning should not depend on a connection.

Built for Idutywa

Designed with local learners and teachers, not for them.

Learning that’s fun

Games that help learners stay motivated.

Meet the founder

Siphelele is an Entrepreneurial Leadership student at African Leadership University and the founder of FemConnect.

A school holiday trip to the Eastern Cape in 2015 showed how hard it is to study without internet. Siphelele wants to build the tutor that was missing, together with learners and teachers in Idutywa.
